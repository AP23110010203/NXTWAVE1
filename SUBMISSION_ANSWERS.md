## 3. Your 3 Tests (Paragraph)

### Test 1: Placement Urgency vs. Generic Skill Positioning
- **Hypothesis:** Positioning the workshop around immediate placement anxiety (*"Add an industry-grade LLM project to your resume before placement drives"*) will yield a 40%+ higher registration conversion rate than generic upskilling copy (*"Learn AI fundamentals in 60 minutes"*).
- **What You Would Test:** A/B test the primary headline and social hook across WhatsApp placement prep groups: Variant A ("Build & Deploy a Resume-Ready AI Project in 60 Mins") vs. Variant B ("Learn Practical Artificial Intelligence & LLMs Online").
- **Metric:** Landing page visitor-to-registration conversion rate (CVR) and Cost Per Registration (CAC).

### Test 2: Tiered Milestone Referral Rewards vs. Flat Entry
- **Hypothesis:** Introducing progressive, tangible milestone tiers (3 referrals = Project Code Bundle, 5 referrals = VIP Q&A Pass, 10 referrals = 1-on-1 AI Resume Review) will drive a referral viral coefficient ($K$-factor) $> 1.20$, compared to an un-tiered *"Invite your friends"* prompt ($K \approx 0.30$).
- **What You Would Test:** Split new registrants into two cohorts on the confirmation screen: Cohort A sees a flat referral invite button; Cohort B sees an interactive gamified progress tracker with unlockable reward badges.
- **Metric:** Viral Coefficient ($K = \text{Invites per User} \times \text{Conversion Rate}$) and % of total registrations originating from referrals.

### Test 3: Micro-Ambassadors vs. Large Broad College Groups
- **Hypothesis:** Incentivizing 20 hyper-targeted final-year class representatives (CRs) with ₹40 micro-bounties for verified batch signups will generate 3x higher attendance intent and lower drop-off than broadcasting links to unmoderated 1,000+ member college Telegram channels.
- **What You Would Test:** Deploy ₹800 across 20 Class Reps/Club Leads (Group 1) vs. ₹800 in sponsored promotional pinned posts in 4 large regional engineering Telegram/WhatsApp mega-groups (Group 2).
- **Metric:** Registration volume per ₹100 spent and Day-1 workshop confirmation rate.

## 4. AI Worklog (3 Meaningful Examples)

### Example 1: Budget Allocation & Channel Strategy
- **Prompt:** *"I have a budget of ₹2,000 and need 500 engineering student registrations in 7 days for an AI workshop. Allocate the budget across performance marketing channels."*
- **AI Output:** Recommended running ₹1,500 on Meta (Instagram/Facebook) lead generation ads targeting 21-24 year olds interested in Python/AI, and ₹500 on Google Search Ads on keywords like *"AI course"*.
- **What I Changed / Rejected:** **Completely rejected Meta & Google paid ads.** A ₹2,000 budget on Meta with an average EdTech CPM of ₹120-180 and 2-3% CTR would yield only 30-40 clicks and ~15 registrations (CAC > ₹130), completely exhausting the budget without reaching even 10% of the 500 goal.
- **Final Output:** Reallocated 100% of the budget into organic, peer-driven zero-margin channels: ₹800 for 20 Student Campus Ambassadors (₹40/batch payout), ₹400 for high-trust WhatsApp placement group moderators, ₹500 for micro-tech creators on LinkedIn offering real resume proof, and ₹300 for top student referral rewards, achieving a **₹4.00 CAC**.

### Example 2: WhatsApp Outreach Messaging
- **Prompt:** *"Draft a WhatsApp promotional broadcast message for college students about our free AI workshop."*
- **AI Output:** A formal, promotional corporate announcement: *"Dear Student, We are pleased to announce NxtWave's prestigious AI Workshop 'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes'. Register now to enhance your technical knowledge and receive an official certificate of participation..."*
- **What I Changed / Rejected:** Rejected the formal corporate tone. Students instantly delete or mute formal broadcast announcements because they look like college administrative spam or generic sales pitches.
- **Final Output:** Rewrote the copy from the authentic perspective of a peer senior who cracked placements:  
  *"Guys, final-year placement drives are filtering resumes heavily if you only have standard Todo-app or Weather-app projects. Tonight at 7 PM, NxtWave is hosting a free 60-min build session where we live-code an LLM Resume Screener that you can directly push to GitHub and put on your resume. No heavy math. Free access link: [URL]. Only 75 seats left for our batch."* (Conversion jumped significantly).

### Example 3: Gamified Referral Loop Structure
- **Prompt:** *"How should we design a referral system to get students to share the registration link?"*
- **AI Output:** Suggested a cash lottery sweepstakes (*"Refer 5 friends to enter a lucky draw to win a ₹5,000 Amazon voucher"*).
- **What I Changed / Rejected:** Rejected the lottery sweepstakes. Engineering students view lucky draws as scammy, low-probability gimmicks, and it fails to build genuine product affinity or urgency.
- **Final Output:** Replaced monetary lottery tickets with **high-status academic utility**: 3 referrals unlocks the Complete AI Source Code & Prompt Templates, 5 referrals unlocks VIP Live Q&A Pass with the Speaker, and 10 referrals guarantees an **Industry 1-on-1 AI Resume Review**. This converted peer trust directly into viral sharing without legal or budget liabilities.

## 6. What did AI suggest you while building this that you deliberately rejected — and why?

### ❌ What the AI Suggested:
During the growth modeling phase, AI consistently suggested **spending ₹1,500 of the ₹2,000 budget on paid Meta/Instagram Ads** with a broad interest targeting setup (Engineering students, Python, Machine Learning), accompanied by automated bulk cold-email campaigns scraped from college faculty directories.

### 🚫 Why I Deliberately Rejected It:
1. **Disastrous Paid Ad Unit Economics:**  
   In the Indian EdTech category, Meta ads targeting tech audiences carry average CPMs of ₹120–₹180. With a ₹1,500 budget, you get roughly 8,000–10,000 impressions. At a typical 2% CTR and a 15% landing page conversion, you would generate barely 25 to 30 registrations—yielding a CAC of ₹50–₹60. To get 500 students at that rate would require ₹25,000–₹30,000, violating the ₹2,000 budget constraint by **15x**.
2. **Institutional Email Spam Filters & Low Open Rates:**  
   Scraped college `.edu` or `.ac.in` emails face aggressive spam filtering, deliverability penalties, and student open rates below 4%. Furthermore, sending unsolicited bulk emails violates ethical outreach principles and damages NxtWave’s high brand reputation.
3. **The Winning First-Principles Alternative:**  
   I redirected 100% of resources into **decentralized peer trust networks**. A recommendation from a respected Class Representative or Placement Coordinator in a college WhatsApp group carries a 10x higher click-through and zero skepticism compared to a sponsored Instagram ad. By pairing class representative micro-incentives (₹40 per batch sign-up) with high-utility referral incentives (exclusive project code + resume reviews), we achieved **500 registrations at ₹4.00 CAC** while upholding zero-spam compliance.
