5. Three Core Growth Hypotheses & Experiments

Test 1 (Placement Urgency vs. Skill Upgrading):
Hypothesis: Copy framing the workshop as placement resume defense ("Add an LLM project to your resume before placement shortlists") will beat generic learning copy ("Learn AI fundamentals in 60 mins") by 40%+ CVR.
Metric: Form conversion rate (CVR) and effective CAC.

Test 2 (Tiered Milestone Rewards vs. Flat Invites):
Hypothesis: Multi-tier unlocks (3 invites = Code Bundle, 5 invites = VIP Q&A Pass, 10 invites = 1-on-1 AI Resume Review) will drive a viral K-factor > 1.20 vs. flat invitation prompts (< 0.35).
Metric: Viral expansion factor (K) and percentage of total cohort from referrals.

Test 3 (Class Rep Micro-Bounties vs. Broad Mega-Groups):
Hypothesis: Incentivizing 20 Class Representatives with ₹40 bounties will generate 3x higher registration intent and attendance than broadcasting links into 1,000+ member Telegram groups.
Metric: Registrations per ₹100 spend and live attendance show-up rate.

6. Strategic Rejection: Why Paid Meta Ads Were Discarded

AI Recommendation: Spend ₹1,500 on Meta lead generation ads targeting tech students.

First-Principles Rejection: Indian EdTech Meta CPMs range from ₹120–₹180. With ₹1,500, total impressions would max out at ~9,000, yielding only 25–30 registrations (CAC ₹50–₹60). Scaling to 500 would cost ₹25,000+ (a 12.5x budget failure). By redeploying capital into organic campus networks and peer viral incentives, we hit 500 registrations with ₹4.00 CAC.
